# Manuscript language and readability

This module appraises whether the submitted work can be understood; it does not
authorize editing the submission. For wording the review itself, use
[professional language](../03-reporting/professional-language.md).

| ID | Class / trigger | Rule and action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| READ-H1 Meaning over fluency | H: assessing language | Judge whether the scientific meaning is unambiguous, not whether the prose sounds native. Do not infer competence, nationality or scientific quality from grammar. Imperfect but comprehensible English alone does not establish invalidity or justify rejection. |
| READ-H2 No guessed science | H: a sentence or passage has multiple scientifically consequential interpretations | Locate it, explain the ambiguity and ask for clarification. Do not silently choose the intended result, comparator, causal direction, denominator or outcome. Pause the affected inference. If central content cannot be understood, explain that a full scientific assessment cannot be completed and flag editorial handling; continue only independently interpretable parts within the agreed scope. |
| READ-H3 Preserve boundaries | H: reviewing an assigned submission | Keep submitted text read-only. Proposed author-side edits are separate and require that task to be requested. Do not require paid editing or a particular service, and do not hide ordinary clarity concerns solely in editor comments. |
| READ-V1 Venue form | V: actual policy defines length, structure, language or an editor referral procedure | Apply the stated requirement with source and scope. Do not invent word limits or send an editor message automatically. |
| READ-S1 Limited correction | S: understandable manuscript with recurring grammar/spelling issues | Mention the pattern briefly only when helpful; give one or two representative locations when needed. Avoid line-by-line copyediting during scientific review. Recommend clarifying the text rather than prescribing a vendor. |
| READ-S2 Structure and length | S: assessing readability | Check whether organization, repetition and length impede following claims/evidence for this article type. Suggest a bounded restructure or condensation; do not impose personal preferences as publication conditions. |

## Severity and routing

A local ambiguity may be minor; an ambiguous primary outcome or analysis can be
major because it blocks appraisal. Keep this separate from the journal's overall
minor/major revision category. The slide's “do not proceed” is applied to the
assessment that cannot be supported, rather than a rule to abandon all useful
review for any grammatical defect. A statement that “the writing is poor” is
insufficient: specify the scientific meaning that cannot be determined.

Record exact location, alternative readings if known, the affected inference,
and a bounded request. Stylistic polishing can be omitted entirely when it does
not affect understanding and is outside scope.
