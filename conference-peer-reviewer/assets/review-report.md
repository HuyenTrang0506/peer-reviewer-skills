# Working draft for human verification

Replace instructions and adapt to the actual conference edition/track form. Do not invent scores or required fields. Record the submission version, review stage, policy sources, and assessment limits. Remove private handoff instructions before submission.

For drafting decisions, use [Chapter 9: report writing](../references/03-reporting/report-writing.md).
Order concerns by consequence or by manuscript section as useful. Priority follows
the scientific effect; uncertainty omissions and figure discrepancies are not
automatically minor. Distinguish required from optional actions regardless of
major/minor priority; number comments and use truthful page/line/panel locators. Evaluate contribution against the actual article type and
venue criteria, without a universal novelty threshold.

## Author-facing review — readership as specified by venue

### Contribution summary

Describe the problem, approach, evidence, and claimed contribution accurately. Do not infer acceptance or novelty from branding.

### Strengths

List specific supported strengths tied to the contribution and venue criteria.

### Weaknesses and decision-relevant concerns

Use stable IDs such as C1. For each: inspected location; observation and criterion; consequence for the claim; priority; smallest adequate remedy. Distinguish observed error, reporting gap, and unresolved question.

### Questions for author response

Tie each question to a concern ID and say what answer/evidence would affect the assessment. Respect the permitted rebuttal materials and realistic scope of work.

### Optional improvements

Separate helpful suggestions that are not conditions for the recommendation.

### Limitations, ethics, and reproducibility fields

Answer the actual form. Route sensitive details through designated restricted fields. An acknowledged limitation is not automatically grounds for rejection.

## Ratings — readership and scales as specified by venue

| Requested criterion | Exact venue scale/anchor | Proposed rating, if justified | Evidence/rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

- **Overall recommendation:** Use the provided category/scale and explain decision-relevant reasons. If the rubric is unknown, give a provisional qualitative assessment without an invented number.
- **Reviewer confidence, if requested:** Use the venue's definition; state expertise, unchecked proofs/calculations, and unavailable materials. Do not imply actual specialist consultation.
- **Permitted assistance disclosure:** Complete required field(s).

## Rebuttal/discussion update — only at the permitted stage

| Concern ID | Initial concern/rating relevance | Response and permitted new-material location/version | Status and evidence | Effect on rating and reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

State the updated overall assessment and what changed or remained unresolved. Distinguish verified changes from promises. Do not silently move the criteria or require unrelated experiments.

## Restricted AC/PC/ethics note — separate authorized channel

Record relevant conflicts, competence limits, process concerns, or evidence-based integrity concerns. Explain uncertainty and requested handling. Keep ordinary scientific reasons available to authors. Check actual readership before handoff.

## Private handoff checklist — remove before submission

- Correct edition/track, stage, source version, rubric, and audience.
- Scores and confidence match the venue definitions and written reasoning.
- Evidence, locations, priority, and remedy verified by the human reviewer.
- Rebuttal questions are answerable within policy; optional requests are labeled.
- Response updates and rating changes have reasons and stable IDs.
- Disclosures, incomplete checks, and retention rules addressed.
