## Review-process transparency

Record the actual policy and source for each dimension; unknown is not none. These fields adapt ANSI/NISO Z39.106-2023 for local documentation and do not establish compliance or prescribe conference policy.

- Identity transparency: all identities visible / single anonymized / double anonymized / triple anonymized / unresolved; additional role/stage details:

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use source IDs for the actual report/check supplying a concern, not invented expert identities. Persona differences do not establish independent expertise. A single reviewer can use this table without a panel.
