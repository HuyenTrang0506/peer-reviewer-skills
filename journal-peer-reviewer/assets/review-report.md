# Working draft for human verification

Adapt to the venue. Replace field instructions and remove unused sections before handoff. Keep audiences in separate outputs when required. Do not copy the private workbook into the report.

For drafting decisions, use [Chapter 9: report writing](../references/03-reporting/report-writing.md).
Order concerns by consequence or by manuscript section as useful. Priority follows
the scientific effect; uncertainty omissions and figure discrepancies are not
automatically minor. Distinguish required from optional actions regardless of
major/minor priority; number comments and use truthful page/line/panel locators. Evaluate contribution against the actual article type and
venue criteria, without a universal novelty threshold.

## Comments to authors

### Summary and contribution

Briefly describe the question, approach, findings, and contribution without inventing details or announcing a decision.

### Strengths and overall assessment

State supported strengths, central scientific issues, and relevant assessment limits.

### Major comments

Repeat for each supported issue using stable IDs such as M1:

- **Location:** Exact section/page/line/figure/table where available; otherwise a truthful locator.
- **Observation and evidence:** What is reported and the relevant result/criterion; distinguish uncertainty from demonstrated error.
- **Why it matters:** Effect on a central claim, uncertainty, interpretation, ethics, or appraisal.
- **Requested action:** Bounded correction, explanation, analysis, or claim revision, with an adequate alternative where appropriate.

### Minor comments

Number localized corrections with locations and requested actions. Omit if none are supported or state none identified within scope.

### Optional suggestions

Label useful extensions as optional, not conditions for publication.

### Revision assessment — for re-review only

Use the existing issue IDs. For each, give the response and changed-manuscript locations, status (resolved, partly resolved, unresolved, or no longer applicable), and scientific reason. Separate newly discovered central issues and explain their relevance. Do not regenerate the original report unchanged.

## Confidential comments to editor — separate channel

- **Recommendation, if requested:** Venue category; advice to the editor.
- **Rationale:** Central findings, remediability, and applicable venue criteria. Scientific objections also appear in author comments.
- **Limits/specialist needs:** Materials or expertise affecting confidence.
- **Process/integrity concerns, if applicable:** Specific observations, uncertainty, request for editorial handling; no accusation.
- **Conflicts/assistance disclosure:** As required by policy.

## Private handoff checklist — remove before submission

- Human verified the science, locations, and external claims.
- Unsupported criticisms and unfilled instructions removed.
- Required and optional requests distinguishable and proportionate.
- Author reasons and recommendation consistent.
- Confidential matters routed correctly.
- Scope and unperformed checks stated accurately.
- Venue disclosure and retention/deletion requirements satisfied.
