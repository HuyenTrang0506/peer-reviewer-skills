# Sources and provenance

Source access checked on 2026-10-22. This is a source note for an initial methodology, not a systematic literature review or validation study.

## Sources inspected

1. **User-supplied course screenshots**, Sections 1 and 3. Used for topic coverage and report audiences. The course author, publication date, institutional attribution, and Section 2 are not supplied. Do not infer them from branding or references to a vendor.
2. **Bourne PE, Korngreen A. Ten Simple Rules for Reviewers. PLOS Computational Biology. 2006;2(9):e110.** [Full text](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.0020110), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.0020110). Full article retrieved and inspected. An experience-based editorial, not experimental evidence for one optimal review procedure. Rules 3, 6, 8, 9, and 10 support concrete reasons for criticism, flexible reviewing methods, venue anonymity rules, clear point-by-point feedback, and editor notes consistent with the scientific report. Its 2006 guidance does not establish current AI policies.
3. **EQUATOR Network.** [Reporting-guideline library and study-type routing](https://www.equator-network.org/). Homepage retrieved and inspected; it routes trials, observational studies, systematic reviews, diagnostic/prognostic studies, qualitative work, and animal studies to relevant guideline families. Individual checklist versions and extensions were not fetched or audited. Use the current library and venue instructions for an actual assignment. The risk-of-bias and reporting-guideline resources are distinct; reporting completeness is not a validity verdict.
4. **Wong T, Soni RN, Glass C. A Guide to an Effective Peer Review. POCUS Journal. 2025;10(2):7–10.** Published 2025-11-17. [PMC full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12658579/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.24908/pocusj.v10i02.20046). Full text and Tables 1–2 inspected on 2026-10-22. An editorial/practical guide: invitation readiness (expertise, time, conflicts), whole-manuscript then detailed reading, section checks, prioritized report, limited copyediting, and editor-facing recommendation. It is journal-oriented guidance, not a conference standard or controlled validation of reviewing outcomes. Its statement about statistical editors is not assumed to hold for every journal.
5. **ANSI/NISO Z39.106-2023, Standard Terminology for Peer Review.** [Publication record](https://www.niso.org/publications/z39106-2023-peerreview), [standard PDF](https://groups.niso.org/higherlogic/ws/public/download/28900/Z39.106-2023-Peer_Review_Terminology.pdf), DOI [10.3789/ansi.niso.z39.106-2023](https://doi.org/10.3789/ansi.niso.z39.106-2023). Official record, committee page, and full PDF text inspected on 2026-10-22; a rendered definitions page was also checked. §1 limits this edition to external journal-article review; §2 defines four transparency dimensions; Appendix A is informative. The document excludes scientific review criteria and acceptance decision processes from its scope. Details and limits are in [peer-review-standards.md](peer-review-standards.md). No full standard is redistributed here.
6. **NeurIPS 2025 reviewer guidelines.** [Official page](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/ReviewerGuidelines), inspected 2026-10-22. Inspected the review rubric, overall/confidence scales, author-response/discussion process, and publication/readership instructions. Edition-specific example, not a universal conference policy or verified current AI-use authorization for another assignment.
7. **ICLR 2026 reviewer guide.** [Official page](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2026/ReviewerGuide), inspected 2026-10-22. Inspected the staged review/discussion/update process and policies on bounded additional experiments, SOTA, contemporaneous work, and AI disclosures. These policies belong to that edition; recheck the actual assignment's rules.
8. **Additional supplied Elsevier Researcher Academy screenshots.** They explicitly identify the provider and present scholarly-publishing functions, a journal editorial workflow, reviewer qualities, and NISO/STM terminology dimensions. This identifies the newly supplied slides; it does not establish a date or missing sections of the original screenshots. The slide on journal process credits *Peer review: the nuts and bolts*, Sense About Science (2012); the original publication was not retrieved. General statements about “ensuring” validity are interpreted as quality-assurance aims, not guarantees of truth or complete misconduct detection.

## Proposed synthesis rather than quoted standard

The three reading passes, private workbook schema, four issue types, priority/remediability distinction, and report template are this repository's operational design. They are consistent with the inspected guidance but are not presented as verbatim publisher requirements or a universally observed reviewer method.
