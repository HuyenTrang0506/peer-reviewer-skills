# Professional Office Language

Use [the language rules](references/professional-language.md). This small skill
works independently of the journal and conference packages.

## Scope and inputs

Establish audience, purpose, language, requested action, and any known style or
length constraints from the task. Default to the user's language and existing
document context. Ask only when unresolved ambiguity materially changes meaning;
otherwise draft with explicit placeholders where essential facts are missing.

For review comments, improve the expression of supplied findings. Scientific
verification belongs to the reviewer workflow. Editing a report does not
authorize editing its source manuscript or processing it under a new service.

## Rule classes

- **Hard — LANG-H1–H4:** preserve meaning and uncertainty; invent no facts or
  commitments; criticize respectfully and precisely; preserve audience and
  handling limits. Flag consequential ambiguity rather than guess.
- **Conditional — LANG-V1:** follow actual user/venue/organization terminology,
  form and disclosure requirements where applicable.
- **Soft — LANG-S1–S4:** direct structure, plain language, calibrated firmness,
  and minimal editing. Adapt these to the audience and requested rewrite.

## Workflow and output

Identify the intended point and request. Revise for clarity and audience while
preserving technical content, required versus optional actions, and degree of
certainty. Verify numbers, units, negation, names, and commitments against the
input. Return the usable draft in the requested format; add a short note only
for consequential changes or unresolved ambiguity. Do not send or publish it.
